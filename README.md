# What Happened To My Mic?

**What Happened To My Mic? (WHTMM)** is an application designed to make your microphone sound absolutely terrible.
Ever tried to make your mic quality as shitty as possible, looked up a youtube video on how to do it but decided 
not to because it took too long to set up and can not easily be reversed if you want your original audio quality back?

Well in that case, fear no more: Your mic will sound so terrible you (and everyone in your vc) will love it!

## Features

- **Real-time audio processing**: Modify your microphone input while speaking.
- **Microphone selection**: Choose which input device to process.
- **Absolutely no bloat at all**: I mean, bloat is optional, right?
- **System tray integration**: The app is one of those icons you see in the bottom right of your screen if you press the arrow facing up (keeps the program from being intrusive).
- **Quick enable/disable**: Toggle the effect whenever you want in the tray icon menu.

Currently only for Windows, don't expect Linux support for like 10 years (this is a busy student's side project)
